About us

Two founders, one clear idea.

FORA Partners was founded by two business partners who know the same problem from both sides. Good suppliers struggle to get in front of the right retailers, and retailers spend too much time looking for reliable suppliers.

We want to make that first step simple. We listen first, search carefully and only introduce partners who really fit. From the first call to the first order, you always deal with one of the two founders.

The name FORA comes from the Latin word for meeting places. That is what we want to be: the place where the right partners meet.

  • Personal

    You always speak to one of the founders, never to a call centre.

  • Careful

    We check partners before we introduce them, so your time is not wasted.

  • Open

    We work across industries and countries, in German, Hungarian and English.

  • Clear

    You get a clear offer before we start, with no obligation.

FAQ

Questions we often hear.

Who do you work with?

Suppliers who want to sell to retailers, and retailers looking for new suppliers. We are not limited to one industry or country.

How do you find the right retailers?

We start from your products, prices and capacity, then look for retailers whose range and customers fit. We contact them first and only introduce you when there is real interest.

How long does it take?

Most suppliers get their first introductions within a few weeks. It depends on the product and the market.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short briefing call you get a clear offer, with no obligation.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We only share your details with partners you approve, and we sign an NDA on request.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Fónai Levente Richárd and Rada Nándor, as joint controllers for FORA Partners (company in formation), Schönburgstraße 10/4, 1040 Vienna, Austria, info.forapartners@gmail.com. The two founders decide together how your data is used, and you can contact either of them about it.

2. What we process and why

Match requests. When you send the form we process your name, company, work email, country, product category and the details you enter. The form service Web3Forms forwards your request to us by email. We use the data to answer your request and prepare a possible match (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps before a contract) and for our legitimate interest in business communication (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Without these details we cannot handle your request.

Introductions. We pass your details to a potential partner only after you agree to that specific introduction (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future.

Hosting. The site runs on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, time of access and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not receive these logs. GitHub's privacy statement applies to them.

Fonts. The fonts are stored on our own site. Your browser does not connect to Google or any other font service.

3. Who receives data

  • GitHub, Inc., USA, as hosting provider.
  • Web3Forms, India, as form service. It uses Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare for its servers and spam filtering.
  • Our email provider Google (Gmail), Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, where your request arrives.
  • A potential business partner, only with your consent as described above.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

4. Transfers outside the EU

GitHub (USA) and Web3Forms (India) process data outside the EU/EEA. For GitHub this is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. For Web3Forms it is covered by its data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. Google may also process email data in the USA, which is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep data

Requests that don't lead to a contract are deleted 24 months after our last contact. We delete the copy at Web3Forms at the same time, and Web3Forms deletes it after 3 years at the latest. Contract and accounting records are kept as long as the law requires (Austria: 7 years, Hungary: 8 years).

6. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to the email address in the Imprint. We answer within one month.

7. Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority. Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at. Hungary: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest, www.naih.hu.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies, and no social media plugins, videos or maps. Your browser only stores your language choice and that you have seen the cookie notice. This is technically necessary and needs no consent. If we add analytics later, we will ask for your consent first.
